16 October 2026 Version 1.1.0

Database expansion, new materials, and interface improvements

Version 1.1.0 expands the Atlas Explorer catalog to 100 electronic atlases and adds several new supplementary materials, including a step-by-step Analysis Guide. The update also includes minor classification refinements and interface improvements

Classification

Several classification rules and attribute descriptions were refined

  • Attribute descriptions were revised for Content Type and Paper Version
  • For atlases created on the Geoclip Air platform, Geoclip is now listed as the first App Developer
  • The order of values in the Territory attribute was revised: smaller administrative units are now listed before larger ones
Supplementary Materials

Four new materials were added to support the analysis of electronic atlases

  • Analysis Guide, including an option to download a PDF version
  • Atlas Explorer presentation from EuroCarto 2026
  • A brief article on the background and aims of the Atlas Explorer project, published in Abstracts of the International Cartographic Association
  • Article on the classification of electronic atlases by the Method of Page Presentation
Interface

Several interaction and table-display elements were revised

  • New custom cursors were introduced
  • The horizontal scroll arrows were moved to the bottom table panel, while Clear Filters button was moved to the top toolbar
  • Table row highlighting on hover was made more prominent
  • The number of atlases displayed in the table by default was reduced to 20 records
