Atlas Explorer

About

Atlas Explorer is an interactive web catalog of electronic atlases that enables you to search for atlases, explore their structure, and conduct your own comparative and trend analyses. The aim of the Atlas Explorer project is to identify, analyze, classify, and archive electronic atlases in order to preserve, systematize, and expand knowledge about atlases and their diverse metaconcepts

Atlas Explorer classifies electronic atlases by bibliographic, content, and media-cartographic attributes organised into five blocks: general information, interface, information architecture (IA) and navigation, content representation, and functionality. Definitions of all attributes are available in the table header tooltips

The analysis guide, glossary and other useful information can be found in the Supplementary Materials section. Atlas Explorer employs an express analysis approach that usually takes 2–3 hours. While this approach enables the identification of the synthetic attributes, it provides lower analytical depth and reliability compared to the standard model

New atlases and attributes are forthcoming, and your suggestions are welcome!

Enjoy exploring the atlases!

Atlas Criteria

  1. Defined theme and territory
  2. Includes multiple thematic sections (excluding the metaconcept of organizational mechanism)
  3. Includes purpose-built maps or (geo)spatial visualizations
  4. Content is delivered either within the atlas shell or on purpose-built pages (not PDF pages)
  5. Operational and completed product (not a beta version)
  6. Not a catalog geoportal, collection of dashboards, interactive map, (web)GIS or applied CIS

Atlas Definition

Conceptual Definition

Website or application that contains interrelated and coherent visual and geospatially referenced multimedia content organised in a logical structure within a specific topic and spatiotemporal frame to create an environment for (1) knowledge acquisition, (2) making comparisons and establishing relationships, (3) presenting research findings, (4) supporting decision-making, or (5) storytelling

Operational Definition

Website or application that structures and/or unfolds a specific theme with a focus on spatiotemporal aspects

Desktop experience recommended

Atlas Explorer is designed primarily for desktop and laptop screens. On smaller devices, the atlas table and some analytical tools are unavailable.

For full access to the catalog and comparison tools, please open the website on a screen with a resolution of at least 1368 × 768.
