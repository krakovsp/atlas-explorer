Electronic Atlases: Development Trends and Research Methodology

Book Published: December 30, 2025

The book presents a periodization of electronic atlas (EA) development and identifies metaconcepts of atlas applications. It refines the classification of EAs, clarifies the concept of the EA, and outlines current research approaches and methods. The book provides a detailed characterization of four core EA components as end-user applications: interface, information architecture and navigation, content representation, and functionality. It also introduces and validates a methodology for media-cartographic analysis of EAs through empirical case studies of the European Atlas of the Seas and the Czech Historical Atlas.

Revisiting the Electronic Atlas Concept: From Theoretical Model to Metaconcepts of Atlas Applications

Article Published: June 15, 2026

The article introduces the concept of ‘atlas metaconcept’ and proposes a framework for describing the metaconcepts of electronic atlases. It develops a typology of nine metaconcepts and a matrix of indicators for their identification based on a media-cartographic analysis of 100 electronic atlases. Each metaconcept is accompanied by a definition, a brief description, and examples of atlases.
