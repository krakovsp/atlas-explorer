Site Information & Credits

Project & Authorship

Atlas Explorer is a non-commercial research project independently designed, developed, and maintained by Stanislav Krakovskyi, a Researcher at the Department of Cartography, Institute of Geography of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine.

Technology

The website is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Interactive data tables are implemented using Tabulator 5.5, an open-source JavaScript library distributed under the MIT License.

AI-assisted Development

ChatGPT and Google Gemini were used as supporting tools during code development, debugging, and interface refinement. The project concept, research methodology, data structure, content, and final implementation decisions remain the responsibility of the author.

Visual Materials

Unless otherwise credited, illustrative images used on the website were generated with ChatGPT. Non-AI visual materials include the logo of the Institute of Geography of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine and the cover of the author's monograph Electronic Atlases: Trends in Development and Research Methodology , both designed by V. Horobchenko and used with permission.

These data help assess the geographic reach of Atlas Explorer and understand how the website is used.
